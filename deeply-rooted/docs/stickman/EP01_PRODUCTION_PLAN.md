## PHASE 1 — Script analysis

**Main message:** a life that didn't follow the plan isn't a failed life. It's an unfinished story. Growth is quiet, timelines are invented, loss deserves to be felt, and faith lets you take the next step without seeing the whole road.

**Emotional arc (colour follows it):**

| Act | Time | Emotion | Palette |
|---|---|---|---|
| I · The question | 0:00–0:56 | Disorientation → quiet disappointment | Deep navy, muted blue, desaturated teal |
| II · Invisible timelines | 0:56–2:07 | Comparison → release | Indigo → teal with warm highlights |
| III · Loss | 2:07–3:00 | Grief, honestly held | Muted violet, cool blue, low saturation |
| IV · Quiet growth | 3:00–4:05 | Courage → permission → hope | First amber → golden light |
| V · The bigger picture | 4:05–5:07 | Surrender → trust | Dawn ivory, soft gold, calm teal |
| VI · Still unfolding | 5:07–6:03 | Peace → forward momentum | Warm amber, cream, natural teal |

**Recurring metaphors (one thread, not a new symbol per sentence):**
- **The paper map:** the plan. He carries it, follows it, and finally lets it go.
- **His imagined self:** a luminous figure walking ahead. He chases it, loses it, and at the end walks *with* it.
- **Roots beneath the surface:** growth no one sees. This is the channel's signature image.
- **Doors, seasons and fog:** used once each, never repeated.

**Turning points:**
1. 0:43 "But life had other plans": the road ends.
2. 1:51 "You are a human being, not a project": he finally stops running.
3. 2:43 "Don't let one painful chapter…": the first warm light.
4. 3:16 "You are allowed to begin again": letting go of the map.
5. 4:13 the Qur'an verse: surrender and perspective.
6. 5:41 "Your story is still unfolding": the world opens ahead of him.

**Moments that need visual silence (no new action, let it land):**
- after "…and wondered how you ended up here?";
- after "…not a project that needs to be completed by a certain age.";
- across "…that you hoped, and that it hurts." (a static hold);
- after the verse;
- after "…your story is still unfolding."

**Emotional ending:** he's no longer chasing the version of himself he imagined. They walk together. Beneath his steps, unseen, the roots keep spreading.

## PHASE 4 — Animation planning (how stills become cinema)

- **2.5D camera:** every environment is built as 3–6 depth layers, plus true ground planes where the camera dollies. Real parallax, depth of field and rack focus come from the existing engine.
- **Character:** pose-key animation that blends between library poses (`blendPose`). The walk and run cycles are reused everywhere. A breath cycle is always on, and silent beats get a slow weight shift. No bouncing.
- **Environmental motion as reusable systems:** fog planes, wind fields (grass, trees, leaves; the figure itself reacts to wind only through posture), particle emitters (rain, snow, leaves, blossoms, fireflies, dust), animated light (sun position, window glow, lantern flicker) and water (waves, ripples).
- **Signature transitions built once and reused:**
  - the fog wipe;
  - light leaking under the door;
  - the tilt down into the earth (roots), used three times as the episode's spine;
  - the map unfold (SEQ 21);
  - time-lapse seasons (SEQ 14, 20).
- **Efficiency:**
  - 12 environments × time-of-day palettes;
  - one character rig;
  - 6 particle systems;
  - one camera system.
  - No per-sentence assets.
