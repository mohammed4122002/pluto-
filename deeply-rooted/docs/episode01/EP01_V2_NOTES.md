The cut falls in the silences around it (3.4 s and 5.4 s), and every other word is untouched, unstretched and in its original order. It is the only change to the narration. It is listed in `placement.json` and can be restored there.

- **Subtitles:** the "Episode One" cue is removed from the SRTs as well.

## 3 · Continuous soundscape

- **No restarts:** every ambience bed (lake, rain, wind/air, dawn lake) now plays continuously for the whole film, as a seamless loop (crossfaded loop point). Nothing is restarted at a cut.
- **Glides, not switches:** each shot only sets the target mix for its place, and the mix glides between places over about 4 s, centred on the picture transition.
- **A constant floor:** a quiet wind/air layer runs under every place, so a change of place never drops the floor.
- **Matched levels:** all beds are matched in loudness, so perceived background level stays constant. The rain bed was about 5 dB louder before.
- **Gentle ducking:** the old ducking switched ×0.75 on every phrase. It is replaced by a gentle −1.3 dB dip that follows a smoothed (about 1.7 s) envelope of the voice. There is no pumping.
- **Voice:** unchanged, with its natural pauses. No music and no added effects.
- **Mastering:** as before. Compression, then −15 LUFS, true peak −1.5 dBTP.

## 4–5 · Meaning made visible (key changes)

**"You had plans. You had dreams. You imagined a different version of yourself by now."** (0:11–0:23)
- **Plans:** a dotted golden route is drawn out over the water.
- **Dreams:** its stars kindle.
- **"A different version of yourself":** the light gathers at the far end and assembles into his luminous imagined self.
- **"By now":** the self walks on ahead, further out, while he stays where he is.

**"…happier, more successful, more loved."** The imagined self brightens on each word. It is the expectation, growing.

**"Certain people would still be beside you."** Two faint figures stand beside him, and fade.

**"By this age, by this moment, everything would finally make sense."** (0:31–0:36)
- **Time passing:** a whole day and night pass over the lake in seconds, twice. The sky turns to dawn and back, the mist races, the lamp pales, and the light swings his shadow around him.
- **He doesn't move:** time moves, he waits.
- **"Make sense":** stillness returns, and he lowers his head.

**"By this age I should… By now I should…"** (0:59–1:07) Each "should" lights a milestone on the golden timeline just ahead of him. As he walks on, each one slips behind him and cools to grey: a missed milestone.

**"Stop treating your life like a deadline."** The ruler of "should" returns over him on "deadline". He stops and breathes. On "You are a human being, not a project…" it falls apart, tick by tick.

**"People grow differently… some find their direction early…"** Every traveller on the plain carries their own small light, each at their own pace. A fault from v1 is fixed: the foreground grass had been hiding the travellers.

**"When nobody is watching."** The camera rises out of the soil, where the roots are growing, to find him kneeling alone by the sapling. "Choosing to get up" follows, and "That matters too" brightens the roots.

**"You are allowed to outgrow the dreams you once had… walk away…"** The faint glow of the old dream stays behind at the pier's end as he walks toward us, then dissolves.

**"Not every victory is visible."** A small light kindles in his chest that no one else can see.

**"…beautiful pages left to be written."** The road ahead fills with light.

**The verse (Al-Baqarah 2:216).** Calm sky, no action. The light he let go of drifts up into it. The reference "AL-BAQARAH · 2:216 / سورة البقرة · الآية ٢١٦" sits quietly at the top while the verse is read.

## 8 · Verse and translation

- **English:** the narration's English is the Sahih International translation of 2:216 word for word ("But perhaps you hate a thing and it is good for you; and perhaps you love a thing and it is bad for you. And Allah knows, while you know not."). The subtitles follow it exactly.
- **Arabic:** the subtitles use the standard Hafs text with full diacritics: «وَعَسَىٰ أَن تَكْرَهُوا شَيْئًا وَهُوَ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ ۖ وَعَسَىٰ أَن تُحِبُّوا شَيْئًا وَهُوَ شَرٌّ لَّكُمْ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ». The waqf marks are not shown in the subtitles.
  - This environment could not reach an online muṣḥaf (the network blocks quran.com and similar sites). The text is therefore from the production's own copy and **should be checked against a muṣḥaf before publishing**.
- **Arabic translation edits:** two lines were tightened. "الشخص الذي تصير إليه على طول الطريق" and "من تصبح". A native-speaker review is still recommended.
- **Still open: "believe" vs "believed" at 0:32.** The narration says "believe"; the subtitle keeps "believed", from the approved recording. This needs your decision.

## 11 · Two formats from one production

- **One picture system:** every shot is drawn once, in landscape space. The 9:16 composition shows the same shot at 1.4× scale, with its framing chosen *per shot*. Each shot carries a `vx(f)` focus: the Traveller, or the pair of ideas in the shot (him and the imagined self, him and the people beside him, the lit milestones ahead of him).
- **Moving between framings:** the vertical framing glides between shots during the transitions, so it never jumps.
- **Staging for both formats:** shots were staged so the key elements fit both frames. The imagined self, the ghosts and the milestones were moved closer to him for this.
- **The area below the picture:** in vertical, the scene's own foreground continues below the picture (water or ground, mirrored, softened and darkened), drawn in the paint pass. That band is where platforms put their captions and buttons.
- **Vertical subtitles:** the same component and typefaces, laid out for phones. EN 46 px over AR 50 px, balanced wrapping in an 820 px column, block bottom at y = 1460 (above the bottom 24%), centred slightly left of the action buttons.
- **Vertical titles:** the title card, verse reference and end card each have their own vertical layout.
- **Shared:** narration, soundtrack, cue timing, sets, characters and story devices. Only framing, scaling and text layout differ.

## Efficiency

- **No new plates:** v2 painted no new background plates.
- **New devices are small code-drawn pieces:** the plan line, lanterns, the falling ruler, the time-lapse and the vertical framing.
- **Faster paint pass:** the paint pass computes its broad strokes at half resolution. The picture is the same (mean difference 0.04/255) and the pass is about twice as fast, which pays for rendering a second format.
